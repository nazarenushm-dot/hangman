How to play

  • Guess one letter at a time by clicking the keys or typing on your keyboard.
  • Herbie starts out whole, and every wrong guess erases a piece of him. Once he's gone (7 misses), you lose.
  • Words tagged From the headlines come from recent Daily Nebraskan stories. Finish the round to read the story.
  • Stuck? Click Earn a hint and answer a UNL trivia question. Get it right and you'll get a clue. Get it wrong and it counts as a miss. You get one try per word.
